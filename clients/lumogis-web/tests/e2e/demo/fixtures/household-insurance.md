# Home Insurance — Policy Summary (Rivera household)

**Provider:** Northshore Mutual
**Policy number:** NM-4471-882
**Cover period:** 1 Jan 2026 – 31 Dec 2026

## Cover

| Item | Sum insured |
|---|---|
| Buildings | £450,000 |
| Contents | £60,000 |
| Accidental damage | Included |
| Home emergency | Included |

## Excess

- **Standard excess: £250 per claim.**
- Escape of water (burst pipe, leak): £500 per claim.
- Subsidence: £1,000 per claim.

## Making a claim

Call the 24-hour claims line on 0800 555 0142, or file online at northshore-mutual.example/claims.
Keep receipts and photos. Emergency repairs to prevent further damage are covered before assessment.

## Notes for the household

The excess is what we pay before the insurer pays the rest. For most claims that's **£250**; water damage is higher. Renewal is due in December — compare quotes a month before.
