In the harbour town of Port Elowen, the old lighthouse keeper Maren Voss kept a
logbook of every ship that passed. Her neighbour Tobias Hale, who ran the bakery
on Quay Street, supplied the keeper with orange marmalade each Sunday. The
lighthouse stood on Gull Rock, a mile from the Elowen harbour wall.

Every spring the town council, led by Councillor Priya Anand, met in the harbour
office to decide whether the lighthouse lamp should be restored. The engineer
Jonas Berg estimated the work at three months. The marmalade, everyone agreed,
was the best in the county.

When the lamp was finally lit again, Maren Voss wrote a single line in her
logbook: the light is on, and the town is home.
