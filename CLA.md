# Contributor Licence Agreement

Lumogis requires all contributors to sign an Individual Contributor
Licence Agreement (iCLA) before any pull request can be merged.

The CLA grants Lumogis the right to use your contribution under any
licence terms, including terms that differ from the project's current
AGPL-3.0 licence. You retain full copyright in your contributions.

## How to sign

When you open a pull request, the CLA Assistant bot will post a comment
with a link to the signing flow. Sign once and it applies to all future
contributions.

You can also sign in advance:
👉 https://cla-assistant.io/lumogis/lumogis
